MagicByte voicemail greeting
============================
Record this as your voicemail greeting at 316-559-4816.
Keep it under ~45 seconds spoken. Smile while you record it —
it comes through in the voice.

---
"You've reached MagicByte phone repair! The wizard can't come to
the crystal ball right now — he's elbow-deep in somebody's cracked
screen.

But here's the magic trick: TEXT this number with your phone model
and what's broken, and I'll text you back with a price, usually the
same day. Repairs happen Tuesday through Saturday.

After the beep, leave your name, your phone's model, and what's
wrong with it!"
---

Notes:
- Calls are never answered live on purpose — the greeting says so
  up front, then gives the faster path (text).
- If you ever get a short custom domain, add one line:
  "…or find MagicByte online at magicbyte.example."
