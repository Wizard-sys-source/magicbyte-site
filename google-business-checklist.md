# Google Business Profile — video verification checklist

Only you can do this one: Google requires the business owner to film it,
and it must show you, your gear, and your work area. Film one continuous,
unedited video (usually 1–3 minutes) in the Google Business app when it
prompts you.

## Shot list

1. **Your face + business name.** Say "I'm Jonathan with MagicByte phone
   repair in Wichita, Kansas" on camera.
2. **Your tools and stock.** Show your repair kit, the iPhone screens you
   keep in the van (12 / 13 / 14), and any signage or the wizard outfit
   if it's handy — it proves you're a real operation.
3. **Your vehicle.** Walk around the car/van you use for mobile repairs.
   Business signage on it helps but isn't required.
4. **Your service area.** A street sign, landmark, or recognizable Wichita
   spot. As a mobile business you'll set a *service area* (not a storefront
   address) after verification, so this just proves you're really here.
5. **A repair in progress or a finished job** (optional but strong). Even a
   demo phone with the screen off and tools laid out reads as legitimate.

## Tips

- Film in daylight, one continuous take — no cuts or edits.
- Make sure the business name is visible *somewhere*: say it, show it on
  a sign, your shirt, or the van.
- After you're verified, set the profile as a **Service Area Business** so
  your home address stays hidden and only "Wichita and surrounding area"
  shows publicly.
- Upload through the Google Business Profile app/website when it asks for
  video verification — don't email it anywhere.
