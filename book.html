How payment works

You should never have to pay a stranger the full amount before meeting them. Here's exactly how it works, and why.

✅

Part already in stock

No payment upfront, ever. I show up, do the repair, you pay on completion — card tap or cash. Check stock status first.

📦

Part needs ordering

A deposit equal to the part's exact cost is required to place the order — never the full invoice, never labor. Labor is still paid on completion only.

↩️

If you need to cancel

Cancel before the part ships: full refund, no questions. Cancel after it's shipped: a $10 cancellation fee applies (covers the non-returnable order), the rest is refunded.

Payments run through Stripe/Square, not cash apps — that means a real receipt and buyer protection on their end, not just my word. Most invoices go out directly by text or email once a repair's quoted, but you can also pay straight from here.
