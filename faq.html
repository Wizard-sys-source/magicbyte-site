The Oracle's Answers

Knowledge is power.

Total transparency is the foundation of good magic. Here are the answers to the most common questions travelers ask before handing over their artifacts.

Are you a real wizard, or just a weird tech guy? +

A healthy mix of both. The wizard persona makes a stressful situation fun, but my repair standards are dead serious. I believe in treating your device like a vital piece of machinery, not a disposable toy.

What if the mending spell fails? (Refunds) +

If I attempt the repair and the device is truly beyond saving, you pay $0. If you cancel before a special-order part ships, you get a full refund. If it has already shipped, a tiny $10 cancellation fee applies, and the rest of your gold is returned.

How does the 30-Day Ward (Warranty) work? +

Every repair is magically sealed with a 30-day guarantee on both the component and my labor. If something related to the repair fails in that window, I will recast the spell and fix it at no charge.

Where do the rituals take place? +

I travel to you anywhere in the Wichita area—your home, your office, or a public meeting spot like a local tavern (coffee shop). For first-time travelers, I usually suggest a public meeting spot for everyone's comfort.

Can I just call you on a standard telephone? +

Calls to my line go directly to the void (voicemail). I do not take live calls so I can focus purely on repairs. Texting, emailing, or speaking into the Consultation Orb are the fastest ways to summon me.
