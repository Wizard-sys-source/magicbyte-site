FAQ

Questions answered.

Are you a real wizard, or just a weird tech guy?+

A healthy mix of both. The wizard persona makes a stressful situation fun, but my repair standards are dead serious. I believe in treating your device like a vital piece of machinery, not a disposable toy.

How long does a repair take?+

I quote about 1:30 (an hour and a half) for most repairs — screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras — and most finish sooner than that. The one exception is back glass on iPhone X through 14, which takes at least two days since it requires laser separation I don't have equipment for yet. Everything else, including back glass on Android and iPhone 15+, is same-day. If a part needs to be ordered first, add 3–5 days for shipping.

Is there a warranty?+

Yes — every repair is covered for 30 days on both the part and the labor. If something related to the repair fails in that window, it's fixed at no charge.

Where do repairs happen?+

I come to you anywhere in the Wichita area — home, office, or a public meeting spot if you'd rather. For first-time customers, I'll usually suggest meeting somewhere public like a coffee shop or library lot.

What if I need to cancel or want a refund?+

If your part hasn't been ordered yet, you get a full refund, no questions. If it's already been ordered, a $10 cancellation fee applies to cover the non-returnable order, and the rest of your deposit is refunded. You're never charged for labor until the repair is actually done.

Can I just call you?+

Calls to 316-559-4816 go to voicemail — I don't take live calls so I can focus purely on repairs. Text, email, the Consultation Orb, or the booking form all reach me directly and get a faster reply.
