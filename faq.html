FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you a real wizard, or just a weird tech guy?+

A healthy mix of both. The wizard persona makes a stressful situation fun. The repair standards behind it are dead serious — your device is a piece of machinery worth restoring, not a disposable.

How long does a repair take?+

About an hour and a half for most jobs — screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras — and most finish sooner. The exception is back glass on iPhone X through 14, which takes at least two days because it needs laser separation I don't have the equipment for yet. Everything else, including back glass on Android and iPhone 15 and up, is same-day. If a part has to be ordered first, add 3–5 days for shipping.

Is there a warranty?+

Yes. Every repair is covered for 30 days on both the part and the labor. If something related to the repair fails in that window, I fix it at no charge.

Where do repairs happen?+

I come to you anywhere around Wichita — home, office, or a public spot if you'd rather. For first-time customers I'll usually suggest somewhere public, like a coffee shop or a library lot.

What if I need to cancel, or want a refund?+

If your part hasn't been ordered yet, you get a full refund, no questions. If it has, a $10 cancellation fee covers the non-returnable order and the rest of your deposit comes back. You're never charged for labor until the repair is actually done.

Can I just call you?+

Calls to 316-559-4816 go to voicemail — I don't take live calls, so I can keep my hands on the bench. Text, email, or the orb all reach me directly and get a faster reply.

Do I have to give you my passcode?+

Only if you want me to test the repair, and I never write it down or keep it. After a screen job I need the phone unlocked to check touch, cameras, Face ID and the mics. If you'd rather not, take it back locked and test it yourself before you pay — that's a completely fine choice. More detail on the Trust page.

Will I lose my data? Do you look through my phone?+

No and no. I don't open your photos, messages or apps, and I don't copy anything off the device. Repairs shouldn't touch your data, but hardware work carries risk, so back up before I arrive — I can't take responsibility for data loss.

Are the parts genuine Apple or Samsung?+

No. I fit professional-grade aftermarket parts from MobileSentrix and Injured Gadgets, the distributors most independent shops in the US buy from. On an iPhone that means iOS will log a non-genuine part in Settings, True Tone usually stops working, and battery health may stop displaying. The phone is otherwise completely normal. The full explanation is on the Trust page — read it before you book if that matters to you.

What devices do you work on?+

iPhone, Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel are the bulk of it — screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras and back glass. Other Android phones and tablets depend on whether parts are available for that model, so send me the model and I'll check. I don't do laptops or consoles.

Do you fix water damage?+

Only if the device still shows some sign of life — a flicker, a vibration, anything. If it's completely dead that's board-level work needing a microsoldering bench I don't have, and I'll say so instead of charging you to find out.

Do you work on Face ID and fingerprint sensors?+

They keep working through a screen repair as long as the original sensor assembly transfers across cleanly, which is normal careful handling. What I can't do is repair a Face ID module that's already failed — that's board-level.

What if you can't fix it, or you damage my phone?+

If I can't fix it, you owe nothing for labor. If I damage something during the repair, I make it right — that's what the workmanship half of the 30-day ward is for. I'd rather tell you a job is beyond me before starting than hand you back something worse.

Do I need an appointment?+

Yes, but it's just a text. There's no shop to walk into — I come to you, so we agree a time and place first.

How do I pay, and when?+

You pay when the repair is finished and tested, never before. The one exception is special-order parts, which need a deposit covering the part cost so I'm not out of pocket if the job falls through. I'll confirm which payment methods I take when we set the time.

What areas do you cover?+

Wichita and the nearby towns — Derby, Andover, Haysville, Maize, Goddard, Park City, Bel Aire, Rose Hill and Mulvane. Further out, just ask and I'll tell you honestly whether the drive works.

Still stuck on something?

Ask it directly and I'll answer — no obligation, no quote required.
