The Forbidden Arts (Limitations)

To ensure I only provide fixes that actually last, I do not practice Deep Water Resurrections (unless the device still shows a spark of life) or Microsoldering (board-level chip repair). I focus strictly on modular perfection and glass restoration.

How a job works

1. Book

Send your device, model, and issue through the booking form, or text 316-559-4816.

2. Quote & deposit

You'll get a quote (part cost + labor). If the part isn't in stock, a deposit covers ordering it.

3. Repair

In-stock repairs are usually done same day, on site, wherever works for you in Wichita.

4. Warranty

Every job is covered — see the FAQ for terms.
